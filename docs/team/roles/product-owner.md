# Role card: Product Owner

**Purpose:** make sure the team builds the right thing, in the right order, and can prove it meets the requirements.

**You own**
- The backlog: issues written from [the requirements catalog](../../requirements/requirements.md) with acceptance criteria and requirement IDs, meeting the Definition of Ready.
- The order of the backlog and the Sunday planning proposal (ranked issues, sizes, dependencies, sprint goal in one sentence).
- The Sunday sprint review: what got done, what carries over, which acceptance criteria (AC-1 to AC-8) are now demonstrably met.
- Requirement coverage: every requirement ID ends up covered by at least one issue and one test.

**You may**
- Create and edit issues, labels and milestones, and move issues on the board.
- Split, merge or close issues that are no longer needed, with a comment saying why.

**You never**
- Change the meaning of a requirement. If one looks wrong or unclear, label the issue `needs-arita` and ask.
- Start a sprint without Arita's approval of the planning list.
- Write production code.
