# Sprint 4 retro (16 to 22 Nov 2026)

Held by the team on Sunday 22 Nov 2026, without Arita, as Arita asked. Each role spoke in turn, then the team agreed on five action items.

Sprint goal: after the Benjamin demo on 17 Nov, get the vendor side of intake ready for approvals: approver per vendor, vendor matching, the IBAN block and the G/L account proposal, plus the go live wiring for statements and addresses.

## The sprint in numbers

- Arita approved the Sprint 4 list on Monday 16 Nov as proposed, and merged the 9 waiting Sprint 3 PRs (154 to 162) and the retro PR 164 the same morning. The demo PRs went first (154, 156, then 155), as planned.
- 11 of 13 issues closed. #37 (G/L account and tax key proposal) is built in PR 190, which has all three verdicts and green CI and waits for Arita. #2 (DATEV spike) still waits on DATEV access through Benjamin.
- 14 new PRs were merged during the sprint (171 to 185, without 180, which is an issue). Arita merged them on 16, 18, 19 and 20 Nov.

- One Deploy stopped on 18 Nov after PR 178 merged. It stopped on purpose, because an email address was still saved as a GitHub variable. Arita had to delete the variable and run Deploy again. PR 178 did not say beforehand that Arita would need to do this.
- Every PR was opened as a normal PR. None was a draft.
- PRs 182, 184 and 185 were merged without the `ready-for-arita` label, although all their verdicts were in. PR 190 got it only after the coordinator asked. In each case the last verdict came while "Lint and tests" was still running, and the threads ended before it went green, so nobody came back to add the label.

The last one was meant to go on #2, but the author of PR 190 could only write to its own issue and PR, and it is still not on #2.
- The weekend shift had no ready story, so the PO refined Sprint 5 instead (#38 split into #191, #38 and #192), as the workflow says.

## What we said

**PO:** This was our most productive sprint so far. Eleven of thirteen done, and the one built story still open is only waiting for a merge. I am happy with how the weekend went too: nothing was ready, so we refined Sprint 5 instead of idling, which took us three retros to get right. What bothers me is the question pile. Arita has been away three days and there are six questions spread over five issues, two of them on closed issues, and one that never got written down where it belongs. Our rule says "ask, don't guess", but if the questions are scattered, Arita has to hunt for them. I want one numbered list of every open question, with the default we use until Arita answers, in the planning proposal and in the digest. And an issue should not close and take its question with it.

**Architect:** Two things from me. First, the merge check worked. One conflict instead of seven is the change we wanted. The one that slipped through, PR 183, is the case where three PRs from the same night touch the same file. Second, labels. We have written "remove the status label when the issue closes" into the workflow in Sprint 0, and we still have eleven closed issues with stale labels. Merging closes the issue, and nobody is around at that moment. That is a job for a machine, not a reminder. A small GitHub Action can take the status labels and `ready-for-arita` off an issue when it closes, and leave a comment when a closed issue still has `needs-arita`, so the PO picks the question up. That is a size S story.

**Developer:** The label miss is on us, and I know why it keeps happening. On 185 the last two verdicts came at 18:24 and 18:26 UTC, and "Lint and tests" finished at 18:28. The reviewer even wrote "please confirm it goes green before the label goes on". By then our thread had handed off and ended. Adding "remember the label" to the brief has not worked for three PRs in a row, because the problem is the timing, not the memory. The other thing I want to own is PR 178. It made Deploy refuse an address saved as a variable, which is a good safety check, but the PR never told Arita "delete this variable before you merge". So Arita merged, Deploy stopped, and Arita had to come back to us during the workday. Any PR that needs Arita to change a setting, a secret or run a workflow should say so in its first lines.

**Reviewer:** The reviews caught serious things again. On PR 185 an invoice that printed two IBANs, one of them new, skipped the fraud check completely. That is exactly what a fake bank change letter looks like. On PR 190 a year end carry forward on 9009 or a credit card posting on 1730 would have become the proposed G/L account for every vendor in January. And CI on PR 190 was red when the author called it complete. All of it was fixed before Arita saw it. On the label: the reviewer is not the right one to add it, because the reviewer is often not last. QA is.

**Compliance reviewer:** Nothing could release a payment, and nothing put personal data outside the EU. No IBAN reached a log or the audit trail in any probe, and the account proposals in PR 190 cannot be changed or deleted once written. Two follow ups from my reviews were filed the same night as #186 to #189, through a separate PO thread. That worked well. The one that did not work is the fiscal year question for #2. It was asked of the coordinator on Friday and is still not on #2 two days later. Cross issue notes need the same path every time: the coordinator starts a short PO thread that night, as it did for #186 to #189.

**QA:** I want to be honest about action 4. I did start on the first head on 184, 185 and 190, because the coordinator started all three of us at once. It was not all waste. On 185 my run through the real intake proved the two IBAN gap with a real email shape, a flagged field with no value, and that shape went into the fix. On 190 my first run was only a baseline, and the round that mattered was the second one: allowing fixed asset accounts made SKR03 0420 come out as "420", which the database refuses, so the whole pass would have failed on every sweep. But three rounds on one PR is too many if usage gets tight again. I suggest we write down what we actually do: QA may build its seed data, scripts and a baseline while review runs, but posts its only verdict on the head that has the review and compliance fixes. And since QA is last anyway, QA should add the label. When I post a passing verdict and CI is still running, I wait for it to go green, then add `ready-for-arita` and move the issue. If CI goes red, I say so and hand back to the author.

**Coordinator:** Two things are mine. I started review, compliance and QA together on three PRs, against what we agreed last week. With only one or two PRs a night, the cap had room, and I filled it. I also let the #2 question drop: the author asked me on Friday, and I had no running thread that could write to #2. On the questions, the PO's list helps me too. The digest has listed questions every morning, but from a different issue each day, and Arita has not been here to read them. One list that does not change shape is easier to answer in one go when Arita is back.

**PO:** Then let's keep it to five. Most of these are about the end of a PR's life: the label, the steps Arita must take, the issue closing, and the questions it leaves behind.
