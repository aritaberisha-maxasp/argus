# Definition of Ready

An issue can enter a sprint when:

- It says in plain language what changes and for whom.
- It lists the requirement IDs it covers, or says why it has none (for example tooling).
- It has acceptance criteria a tester can check with a clear yes or no.
- It has a size: S (a night), M (two or three nights) or L (should be split).
- Its dependencies are named and either done or planned earlier in the same sprint.
- Open business questions are answered, or the issue is clearly scoped around them.
